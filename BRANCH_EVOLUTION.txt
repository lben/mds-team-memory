GLOBAL OBJECTIVE

Replace routine manual knowledge administration with reliable automatic
maintenance using pretrained models running locally on CPU. Cover concepts,
aliases, relationships, expertise, graph, search, tags, and question routing.
Apply high-confidence findings automatically; hold uncertain findings for
evidence. Preserve administrator edits and opt-outs. Respect shared-server
limits, support offline deployment, and pass quality and lifecycle checks.
